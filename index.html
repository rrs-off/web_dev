01 — OVERVIEW

Understanding Metabolic Syndrome

Metabolic Syndrome refers to a group of conditions that occur together and increase the risk of cardiovascular diseases, stroke, and type 2 diabetes.

These conditions include elevated blood pressure, high blood sugar, excess abdominal body fat, and abnormal cholesterol levels.

03 — MEDICAL DEVICES

Featured Medical Device

WEARABLE DEVICE

Continuous Glucose Monitor

Continuous Glucose Monitors (CGM) are compact wearable devices that measure glucose levels throughout the day.

The sensor sends measurements to a smartphone or healthcare platform, allowing users to monitor glucose trends in real time.
